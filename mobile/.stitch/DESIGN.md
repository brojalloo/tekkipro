# TekkiPro Mobile Design System

## Visual Identity
**Style**: Vibrant, Premium, Coherent
**Atmosphere**: Trusted Business Partner, Modern African Entrepreneurship

## Design Tokens

### Colors
- **Primary (Action)**: `#4F46E5` (Indigo) - Used for primary buttons, active tabs, and highlights.
- **Secondary (Accent)**: `#F59E0B` (Amber) - Used for badges, warnings, and special callouts.
- **Success**: `#10B981` (Emerald) - For sales totals and positive indicators.
- **Error**: `#EF4444` (Rose) - For stock alerts and debt indicators.
- **Background**: `#F9FAFB` - Light gray for scan-ability.
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF` - Card and modal backgrounds.
- **Text (Primary)**: `#111827` - Maximum contrast.
- **Text (Secondary)**: `#4B5563` - Muted info.

### Typography
- **Headings**: Semibold, heavy-weight sans-serif for impact.
- **Body**: Clean, high-legibility sans-serif.

### Layout & Spacing
- **Border Radius**: `16px` (Generously rounded for a modern app feel).
- **Shadows**: Soft, subtle elevation (Indigo tint in shadows for depth).
- **Safe Area**: Strict adherence to device notches and bottom bars.

## Component Patterns
1. **Cards**: Elevated white cards with subtle border and shadow.
2. **Buttons**: Full-width primary buttons with rounded corners and scale-on-press feedback.
3. **Inputs**: Outlined inputs with Indigo focus state.
4. **Navigation**: Floating bottom bar with iconic clarity.

## Iconography
- Consistent use of **Feather** icons (20px standard).
- Stroke width: `2`.
